## Context

The forces in play *at the time this was decided*. What problem needed solving, what
was non-negotiable, what was unknown. Write for someone reading this in two years
who was not in the room — do not assume today's obvious is still obvious.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated actively: "We use X." One paragraph if possible.

## Limitations

The concrete ceilings — numbers, not adjectives. Storage caps, rate limits, timeouts,
scaling walls. This is the section that tells a future reader whether they have hit
the edge of the decision or merely a bug.
